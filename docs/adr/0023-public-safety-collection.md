# ADR 23: source-bound incident collection on the owned runtime

Status: collection foundation implemented; full coordinated incident workflow remains in progress.

Issue #23 requires search/disaster coordination with explicit human observation and handover. Reuse the owned survey runtime, calibrated ground rays and generic shutter rather than introducing a second mission engine or treating inspection's vertical bridge face as ground terrain.

Incident, revision and visit identity enter actual shutter requests. Visible and numeric thermal evidence share their acknowledged capture scope. A small synthetic scene provides independent thermal and visible values without exporting truth labels as detections. No-observe masking happens before shutter storage, with explicit unavailable support.

The owned runtime accepts bounded link/battery observations before actual supervisor and mission decisions. Immutable incident revisions cancel the accepted epoch and require a new pilot decision. They do not mutate a graph in place, reset spent endurance or grant authority. Capture/observation events are emitted causally during virtual execution; real transport and authenticated coordination remain subsequent work.

The deterministic pinned collection profile supports full owned re-execution for bounded source consistency. Exact-byte private artifacts reuse the contributed Stacks completion-manifest writer. Source replay and hashes do not establish producer identity, lawful purpose, physical thermal accuracy, incident RBAC, retention deletion or team acceptance.

Native Stacks commands expose the library. The next implementation dependency is persisted incident scope and native authenticated Actions, followed by observation/review, retention and atomic two-phase handover. Keep the original three acceptance requirements and all hardware/operational qualification visible until verified.
